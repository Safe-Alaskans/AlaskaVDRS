In the afternoon hours, the 30-year-old female victim was found deceased by LE after a welfare check. EMS responded and attempted lifesaving interventions, but after 30 minutes the victim was pronounced. A shotgun was found in between the victims’ legs while she was seated on a sofa with a self-inflicted gunshot wound to the head. The victim was last known alive by her brother when she texted him 2 hours prior to the incident about her plans to commit suicide. The brother then called 911 to check on the victim. The victim had recently endured a breakup with her partner, and they were living in separate accommodations. The victim had a medical history of chronic lumbar pain, PTSD, and anxiety. The victim had no known prior attempts or ideations. The body was sent to the medical examiners. It was determined the cause of death was a self-inflicted gunshot wound to the head. The manner of death was classified as a suicide. The postmortem toxicology showed the victim had a 0.250 BAC. No other circumstances are known.
