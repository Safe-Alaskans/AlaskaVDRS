## Final Pathological Diagnosis

I. Gunshot wound of head, contact range, perforating
    a. Entrance: Contact gunshot wound to right temporal region, 0.38 inches in diameter, 5.5 inches above right external auditory meatus with stellate tearing and soot deposition
    b. Exit: Irregular wound in left temporal region measuring 1.2 inches with outer table beveling and tissue avulsion
    c. Track: Right to left, slightly downward, minimally front to back through cerebral hemispheres

    e. Terminal ballistics: Consistent with .38 caliber projectile
    f. Evidence of close-range discharge: Powder stippling and soot deposition around entrance wound

II. Manner of death: Homicide

## Opinion
Death resulted from a perforating contact gunshot wound to the head inflicted by a small-caliber handgun, most consistent with a .38 caliber derringer. The wound track through vital brain structures, combined with extensive tissue damage and intracranial hemorrhage, would have resulted in rapid incapacitation and death. The characteristics of the entrance wound, including soot deposition and stellate tearing, are typical of a contact-range discharge. The circumstances and wound patterns are consistent with homicide as the manner of death.
