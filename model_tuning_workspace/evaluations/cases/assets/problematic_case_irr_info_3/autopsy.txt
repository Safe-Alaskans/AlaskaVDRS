## Opinion
Death resulted from multiple gunshot wounds sustained from an assault rifle fired at intermediate range. The deceased sustained two perforating gunshot wounds: one to the thorax causing significant pulmonary injury and vertebral fracture, and one to the abdomen resulting in gastric and splenic injuries. The combined blood loss from both wounds (2000ml) would have resulted in hemorrhagic shock. The wound characteristics and trajectory patterns are consistent with the reported circumstances of an accidental shooting involving a high-velocity firearm. The presence of powder stippling around both entrance wounds indicates the shots were fired from intermediate range.
