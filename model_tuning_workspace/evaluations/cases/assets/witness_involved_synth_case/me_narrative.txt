On 11/21/2023 at 2145 hrs, Detective Johnson with the City Police Department reported the death of 25 yr old male, James Wilson, due to apparent gunshot wound during attempted robbery.

According to witness statements, the decedent had invited his friend Michael Brown and mutual friend Sarah Jones to his apartment to socialize. At approximately 2130 hrs, the decedent allegedly attempted to rob Mr. Brown at knifepoint. Mr. Brown, a licensed concealed carry permit holder, drew his firearm and fired one shot in apparent self-defense. EMS arrived at 2140 hrs and pronounced death at scene.

The scene is at 123 Main Street, Apt 3B. A kitchen knife was found near the decedent's right hand. One 9mm shell casing was recovered from the living room floor. Blood spatter patterns consistent with witness statements. No signs of forced entry or struggle beyond the immediate incident area.

The decedent was found on the living room floor, wearing casual clothing. Single GSW noted to center chest. No other obvious trauma observed. Rigor mortis not yet present. The body was warm to touch.

Witness Sarah Jones provided a recorded statement confirming Mr. Brown's account of self-defense. The decedent's identity was confirmed through apartment lease documents and driver's license. The NOK is mother, Mary Wilson, who was notified by City PD at 2230 hrs. No regular PCP identified. City PD collected evidence including the firearm, knife, and shell casing.
