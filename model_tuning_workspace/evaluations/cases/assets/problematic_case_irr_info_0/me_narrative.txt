49 year old female found deceased at St. Mary's Church in downtown Atlanta. The decedent was discovered by the property manager on Saturday morning at approximately 0930 hours, estimated 18-20 hours postmortem. The body was found in athletic attire with a single penetrating wound to the anterior chest wall consistent with a scalpel-type instrument. Multiple surgical scars were noted on examination. Scene investigation revealed signs of forced entry and robbery. The decedent was a regular church member and had access to the premises. Personal effects and valuables were missing from both the victim and church premises. Manner of death is homicide.
