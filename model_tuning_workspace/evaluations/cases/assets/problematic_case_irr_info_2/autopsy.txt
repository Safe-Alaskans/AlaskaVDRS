## Opinion
Based on the autopsy findings and reported circumstances, this individual sustained non-fatal blunt force trauma to the right forearm consistent with a single impact from a chain-like object. The injury pattern, tissue reaction, and coloration confirm this was sustained while the individual was alive. Further investigation into the complete circumstances and potential underlying medical conditions would be warranted to establish the definitive cause of death.
