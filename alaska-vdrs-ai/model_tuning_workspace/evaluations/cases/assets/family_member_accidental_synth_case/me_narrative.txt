45 yr old male, med hx gambling addiction and hypertension. Found deceased in family home with head trauma. SMEO. LADS notified on 11/21/2023 at 2300 hrs.
Addendum by CH: Dr. Jane Smith approved LADS referral.

On 11/21/2023 at 2145 hrs, Officer Johnson with the County Sheriff's Department reported the death of 45 yr old male, Robert Wilson, due to apparent blunt force trauma during family altercation.

The medical history shows treatment for compulsive gambling disorder and hypertension. The decedent had reportedly lost significant family savings gambling earlier that day, leading to a heated argument with his brother James Wilson at the family residence. During the altercation, decedent was allegedly pushed and struck his head on the corner of a marble kitchen counter. Brother immediately called 911 at 2130 hrs. EMS arrived at 2140 hrs and pronounced death without intervention due to obvious signs of death.

The scene is in the kitchen of 123 Oak Street. Blood spatter was noted on the kitchen counter corner and surrounding floor area. Multiple gambling receipts and empty wallet found on dining room table. No weapons were found.

The decedent was found supine on the kitchen floor, fully clothed. Obvious trauma noted to right temporal region with associated hemorrhage. Rigor mortis was 2+ in jaw and neck. The body was warm to touch.

The decedent's identity was confirmed through family members present and ID in his wallet. The NOK is wife Sarah Wilson, who was notified at scene. PCP is Dr. Thompson at County Medical Group, records are being requested. James Wilson was taken into custody by County Sheriff's Department for questioning. County CSI was requested to process scene and transport decedent to SMEO for further examination. SMEO. LADS was notified on 11/21/2023 at 2300 hrs.
