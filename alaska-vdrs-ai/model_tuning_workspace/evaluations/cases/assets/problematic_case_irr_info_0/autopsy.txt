## Opinion
Death resulted from a penetrating thoracic injury caused by a sharp-force trauma, specifically a single thrust with a scalpel-type instrument. The absence of defensive wounds and hesitation marks, combined with the singular, precisely placed wound, is consistent with the reported circumstances of homicide. The cause of death is attributed to exsanguination and cardiac injury due to the penetrating thoracic wound.
